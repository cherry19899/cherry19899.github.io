Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Work Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is a freelance marketplace built on Pi Network. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application through the Pi Browser.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Pi Username: Obtained through Pi Authentication SDK when you sign in. This is your unique identifier on our platform.
  • Pi User ID: A unique identifier provided by Pi Network for authentication purposes.
  • Job and Escrow Data: Information you voluntarily provide when posting jobs, applying for jobs, or creating escrows.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you send through our in-app chat feature.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Government-issued IDs
  • Financial information (except Pi transaction data via Pi SDK)
  • Cookies or tracking data

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for:

  • User authentication via Pi Network
  • Displaying your username on job postings and applications
  • Processing Pi payments for connects purchases
  • Managing escrow transactions between clients and freelancers
  • Enabling chat communication between parties

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only data sharing occurs:

  • With Pi Network for authentication and payment processing
  • Publicly displayed username on job listings (as intended by the platform)

6. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. All job posts, applications, escrow data, and chat messages are retained as long as necessary for the platform to function. You can request data deletion by contacting us.

7. Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all communications and secure authentication via Pi Network's SDK.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes through the app.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Pi Browser or reach out via the Work Pro platform.

11. GDPR / Data Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, and export your personal data. You can request any of these via the in-app chat or by emailing us. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Anonymized analytics data may be retained after your account is deleted.

12. Data Retention

Active data (jobs, messages, escrow records) is retained for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, this data is retained for 1 year to support dispute resolution, then permanently deleted. Backups are retained for 30 days before being deleted.

13. Data Breach

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

14. International Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in our hosting provider's region. By using Work Pro, you consent to this transfer of data outside your own country, where applicable.
